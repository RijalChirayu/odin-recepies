Simple Rajma Dal recepie.

Rajma dal or redbean curry is a curry which is mainly eaten in South Asia.

Ingredients needed
  • Red beans
  • Oil
  • Jeera
  • Turmeric
  • Salt
  • Tomato
  • Water

The steps to cook:

  1. First soak rajma or red beans for a whole day.
  2. Then get a rice cooker and heat it.
  3. After it heats pour some oil.
  4. After few minutes pour jeera on oil along with chili and onion.
  5. When onion changes its colour pour in some turmeric poweder.
  6. After half a minute pour salt and tomato together.
  7. Then stir the paste well till tomato dissolves.
  8. Pour in the red beans along with water as needed.
  9. Close the lid and let it cook for about 10 whistels.
  10. Its ready to serve after the vapour evaporates through whistel.

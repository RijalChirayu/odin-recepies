The recepie for a simple omelete is as follows:


  1. Heat a frying pan on stove.
  2. After half a minute pour just spoonful of oil.
  3. Again after half a minute pour some salt on the oil and shake the pan so that salt mixes well.
  4. Get an egg and break it and just pour.
  5. At this point the pan is hot enough that egg won't stick to it so keep on shaking the pan.
  6. After a minute turn over the omelete to another side.
  7. Then another side will also be cooked and the omelete is ready.
